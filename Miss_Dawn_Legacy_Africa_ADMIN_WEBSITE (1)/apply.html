👑 Eligibility Requirements

Miss Dawn Legacy Africa — All Divisions

  1. Age: Must meet the age requirement for your chosen division.
  2. Marital status: Must never have been married.
  3. Parenthood: Must never have given birth or been pregnant.
  4. Tattoos: Must have no visible tattoos.
  5. Piercings: No visible facial piercings, apart from conventional ear piercings.
  6. Appearance: Must maintain a neat, well-groomed and presentable appearance at all times.
  7. Representation: Must be willing to represent the values, image and reputation of Miss Dawn Legacy Africa.
  8. Criminal record: Must have no criminal convictions or ongoing criminal proceedings involving serious offences.
  9. Availability: Must be available for all compulsory pageant activities and official appearances.
  10. Honesty: Must provide truthful and accurate information during registration. Any material misrepresentation may result in disqualification.
